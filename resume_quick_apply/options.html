3. One-time application defaults

These are common questions a résumé normally cannot answer. Leave anything blank if you prefer to answer it manually.

4. Optional demographic answers

These answers are voluntary and sensitive. Resume Quick Apply will never infer them from your résumé or learn them from an application. It only attempts to fill a demographic question when you explicitly save the matching answer below. If a site's wording is ambiguous or cannot be matched safely, the field stays blank.
